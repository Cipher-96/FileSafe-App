About FileSafe

FileSafe is a privacy-first public utility designed for converting office files, PDF documents, and images without a login or tracking.

Files are processed securely in temporary storage and deleted after conversion. We do not collect personal data or create user accounts.

Privacy commitment:
  • No login
  • No tracking
  • No account database
  • Temporary file cleanup
